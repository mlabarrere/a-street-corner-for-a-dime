Absence from OCR is recorded as `not found`, never as evidence of absence. Aggregate
model-reading results are recorded in `model_reading_sensitivity.md`; they are
exploratory sensitivity inputs, not independent observers or historical sources.

the 62 opening codes and the reference corners; it is not generated by the code.
